# S1200 StealBit (Malware)

**Malware de MITRE ATT&CK** S1200.

- **Plataformas:** Windows

StealBit is a data exfiltration tool that is developed and maintained by the operators of the the LockBit Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) and offered to affiliates to exfiltrate data from compromised systems for double extortion purposes.
