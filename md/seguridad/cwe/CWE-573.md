# CWE-573: Improper Following of Specification by Caller

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-573** (Class).

The product does not follow or incorrectly follows the specifications as required by the implementation language, environment, framework, protocol, or platform.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-710](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-710.md)

## Consecuencias

- Quality Degradation: Unintended behaviors may result, possibly leaving the system vulnerable to any number of exploits.

## Cómo evitarlo

- When leveraging external functionality, such as an API, it is important that the caller does so in accordance with the requirements of the external functionality.
