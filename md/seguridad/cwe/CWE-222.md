# CWE-222: Truncation of Security-relevant Information

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-222** (Base).

The product truncates the display, recording, or processing of security-relevant information in a way that can obscure the source or nature of an attack.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-221](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-221.md)

## Consecuencias

- Hide Activities: The source of an attack will be difficult or impossible to determine. This can allow attacks to the system to continue without notice.
