# CWE-1104: Use of Unmaintained Third Party Components

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-1104** (Base).

The product relies on third-party components that are not actively supported or maintained by the original developer or a trusted proxy for the original developer.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-1357](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-1357.md)

## Consecuencias

- Reduce Maintainability: Relying on unmaintained components makes it difficult or impossible to fix significant bugs and vulnerabilities, can render code obsolete, and undermine security by complicating maintenance and increasing the risk of new vulnerabilities.
