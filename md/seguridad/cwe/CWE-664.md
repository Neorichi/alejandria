# CWE-664: Improper Control of a Resource Through its Lifetime

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-664** (Pillar).

The product does not maintain or incorrectly maintains control over a resource throughout its lifetime of creation, use, and release.

## Consecuencias

- Varies by Context: When code does not follow the instructions for resource management, it can lead to unexpected behaviors and potentially exploitable states.
