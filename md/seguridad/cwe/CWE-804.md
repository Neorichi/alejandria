# CWE-804: Guessable CAPTCHA

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-804** (Base).

The product uses a CAPTCHA challenge, but the challenge can be guessed or automatically recognized by a non-human actor.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-863](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-863.md), [CWE-1390](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-1390.md)

## Consecuencias

- Bypass Protection Mechanism: When authorization, authentication, or another protection mechanism relies on CAPTCHA entities to ensure that only human actors can access certain functionality, then an automated attacker such as a bot may access the restricted functionality by guessing the CAPTCHA.
