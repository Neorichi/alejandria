# CWE-304: Missing Critical Step in Authentication

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-304** (Base).

The product implements an authentication technique, but it skips a step that weakens the technique.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-303](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-303.md), [CWE-573](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-573.md)

## Consecuencias

- Bypass Protection Mechanism: This weakness can lead to the exposure of resources or functionality to unintended actors, possibly providing attackers with sensitive information or allowing attackers to execute arbitrary code.
