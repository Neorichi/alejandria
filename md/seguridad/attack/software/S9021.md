# S9021 DOWNIISSA (Malware)

**Malware de MITRE ATT&CK** S9021.

- **Plataformas:** Windows

DOWNIISSA is a shellcode downloader that has been used by MirrorFace since at least 2022 to deploy payloads, including the LODEINFO backdoor.
