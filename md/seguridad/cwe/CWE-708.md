# CWE-708: Incorrect Ownership Assignment

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-708** (Base).

The product assigns an owner to a resource, but the owner is outside of the intended control sphere.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-282](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-282.md)

## Consecuencias

- Read Application Data: This may allow the resource to be manipulated by actors outside of the intended control sphere. An attacker could read and modify data for which they do not have permissions to access directly.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Periodically review the privileges and their owners.
