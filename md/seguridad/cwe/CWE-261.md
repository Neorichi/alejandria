# CWE-261: Weak Encoding for Password

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-261** (Base).

The product uses a weak or trivial encoding for a password.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-522](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-522.md)

## Consecuencias

- Gain Privileges or Assume Identity

## Cómo evitarlo

- Passwords should be encrypted with keys that are at least 128 bits in length for adequate security.
