# CWE-675: Multiple Operations on Resource in Single-Operation Context

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-675** (Class).

The product performs the same operation on a resource two or more times, when the operation should only be applied once.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-573](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-573.md)

## Consecuencias

- Varies by Context: The consequences will vary depending on how the operation works. This may depend on whether the responsibility for avoiding multiple operations is placed on the developer (which may be more risky), or whether there is built-in protection to prevent multiple invocations or handle them cleanly ("no action" or some kind of error report).
