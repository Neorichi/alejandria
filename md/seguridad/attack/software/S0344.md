# S0344 Azorult (Malware)

**Malware de MITRE ATT&CK** S0344.

- **Plataformas:** Windows

Azorult is a commercial Trojan that is used to steal information from compromised hosts. Azorult has been observed in the wild as early as 2016.
In July 2018, Azorult was seen used in a spearphishing campaign against targets in North America. Azorult has been seen used for cryptocurrency theft.
