# CWE-1475: Incomplete Data/State Propagation

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-1475** (Class).

The product passes control to another component or function, or it performs a state transition, but it does not provide some security-critical data/state that is needed by the receiving component/function.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-664](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-664.md)

## Consecuencias

- Unexpected State: The consequences will vary depending on the type of data/state that is omitted, along with the functionality that the data/state supports.
- Gain Privileges or Assume Identity: If the data/state is needed for a security decision, such as authentication or authorization, then this could allow attackers to bypass the intended protection mechanism.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Clearly identify security-critical data/state structures and ensure that they are propagated when necessary. Be careful of error conditions that could redirect control flow in a way that prevents the propagation from occurring.
