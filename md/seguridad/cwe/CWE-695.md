# CWE-695: Use of Low-Level Functionality

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-695** (Base).

The product uses low-level functionality that is explicitly prohibited by the framework or specification under which the product is supposed to operate.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-573](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-573.md)

## Consecuencias

- Alter Execution Logic: The use of low-level functionality can violate the specification in unexpected ways that effectively disable built-in protection mechanisms, introduce exploitable inconsistencies, or otherwise expose the functionality to attack.
