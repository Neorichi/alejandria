# CWE-697: Incorrect Comparison

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-697** (Pillar).

The product compares two entities in a security-relevant context, but the comparison is incorrect.

## Consecuencias

- Varies by Context: When the comparison is incorrect, it may lead to resultant weaknesses.
