# S9019 PureCrypter (Malware)

**Malware de MITRE ATT&CK** S9019.

- **Plataformas:** Windows

PureCrypter is a fully-featured malware loader, developed by a threat actor called “PureCoder," that has been in use since at least 2021 to distribute a variety of remote access trojans and information stealers.
