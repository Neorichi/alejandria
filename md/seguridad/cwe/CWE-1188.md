# CWE-1188: Initialization of a Resource with an Insecure Default

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-1188** (Base).

The product initializes or sets a resource with a default that is intended to be changed by the product's installer, administrator, or maintainer, but the default is not secure.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-1419](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-1419.md), [CWE-344](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-344.md), [CWE-665](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-665.md)

## Consecuencias

- Varies by Context: The impact of insecure defaults varies widely depending on the functionality that the product controls.
