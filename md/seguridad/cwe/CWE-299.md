# CWE-299: Improper Check for Certificate Revocation

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-299** (Base).

The product does not check or incorrectly checks the revocation status of a certificate.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-295](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-295.md), [CWE-404](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-404.md)

## Consecuencias

- Gain Privileges or Assume Identity: Trust may be assigned to an untrusted entity who somehow obtained the certificate, i.e., the entity is not who it claims to be.
- Other: Data from an untrusted (and possibly malicious) source may be integrated.
- Read Application Data: Data may be disclosed to an untrusted entity that obtained access to the certificate, thereby impersonating a trusted entity, resulting in information disclosure.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Ensure that certificates are checked for revoked status.
- If certificate pinning is being used, ensure that all relevant properties of the certificate are fully validated before the certificate is pinned, including the revoked status.
