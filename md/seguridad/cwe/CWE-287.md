# CWE-287: Improper Authentication

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-287** (Class).

When an actor claims to have a given identity, the product does not prove or insufficiently proves that the claim is correct.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-284](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-284.md)

## Consecuencias

- Read Application Data: This weakness can lead to the exposure of resources or functionality to unintended actors, possibly providing attackers with sensitive information or even execute arbitrary code.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Use an authentication framework or library such as the OWASP ESAPI Authentication feature.
