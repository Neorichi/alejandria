# CWE-636: Not Failing Securely

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-636** (Class).

When the product encounters an error condition or failure, its design requires it to fall back to a state that is less secure than other options that are available, such as selecting the weakest encryption algorithm or using the most permissive access control restrictions.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-657](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-657.md), [CWE-755](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-755.md)

## Consecuencias

- Varies by Context: By entering a less secure state, the product inherits the weaknesses associated with that state, making it easier to compromise. At the least, it causes administrators to have a false sense of security.
- Bypass Protection Mechanism: Intended access restrictions can be bypassed, which is often contradictory to what the product's administrator expects.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Subdivide and allocate resources and components so that a failure in one part does not affect the entire product.
