# CWE-185: Incorrect Regular Expression

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-185** (Base).

The product specifies a regular expression in a way that causes data to be improperly matched or compared.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-697](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-697.md)

## Consecuencias

- Unexpected State: When the regular expression is not correctly specified, data might have a different format or type than the rest of the program expects, producing resultant weaknesses or errors.
- Bypass Protection Mechanism: When the regular expression is used in protection mechanisms such as filtering or validation, this may allow an attacker to bypass the intended restrictions on the incoming data.
- Bypass Protection Mechanism: In PHP, regular expression checks can sometimes be bypassed with a null byte, leading to any number of weaknesses.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Regular expressions can become error prone when defining a complex language even for those experienced in writing grammars. Determine if several smaller regular expressions simplify one large regular expression. Also, subject the regular expression to thorough testing techniques such as equivalence partitioning, boundary value analysis, and robustness. After testing and a reasonable confidence level is achieved, a regular expression may not be foolproof. If an exploit is allowed to slip through, then record the exploit and refactor the regular expression.
