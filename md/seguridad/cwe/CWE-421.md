# CWE-421: Race Condition During Access to Alternate Channel

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-421** (Base).

The product opens an alternate channel to communicate with an authorized user, but the channel is accessible to other actors.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-420](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-420.md), [CWE-362](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-362.md)

## Consecuencias

- Gain Privileges or Assume Identity: This creates a race condition that allows an attacker to access the channel before the authorized user does.
