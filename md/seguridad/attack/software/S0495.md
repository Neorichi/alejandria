# S0495 RDAT (Malware)

**Malware de MITRE ATT&CK** S0495.

- **Plataformas:** Windows

RDAT is a backdoor used by the suspected Iranian threat group OilRig. RDAT was originally identified in 2017 and targeted companies in the telecommunications sector.
