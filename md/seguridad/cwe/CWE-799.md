# CWE-799: Improper Control of Interaction Frequency

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-799** (Class).

The product does not properly limit the number or frequency of interactions that it has with an actor, such as the number of incoming requests.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-691](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-691.md)

## Consecuencias

- DoS: Resource Consumption (Other): This can allow the actor to perform actions more frequently than expected. The actor could be a human or an automated process such as a virus or bot. The consequences vary based on the intended logic of the code. For example, humans might be able to vote multiple times in a poll, or to perform password-guessing attacks without any rate limits.
