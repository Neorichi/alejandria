# CWE-223: Omission of Security-relevant Information

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-223** (Base).

The product does not record or display information that would be important for identifying the source or nature of an attack, or determining if an action is safe.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-221](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-221.md)

## Consecuencias

- Hide Activities: The source of an attack will be difficult or impossible to determine. This can allow attacks to the system to continue without notice.
