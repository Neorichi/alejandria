# CWE-370: Missing Check for Certificate Revocation after Initial Check

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-370** (Variant).

The product does not check the revocation status of a certificate after its initial revocation check, which can cause the product to perform privileged actions even after the certificate is revoked at a later time.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-299](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-299.md)

## Consecuencias

- Gain Privileges or Assume Identity: If the revocation status of a certificate is not checked before each action that requires privileges, the system may be subject to a race condition. Trust may be assigned to an entity who is not who it claims to be. If a certificate is revoked after the initial check, all subsequent actions taken with the owner of the revoked certificate will lose all benefits guaranteed by the certificate. In fact, it is almost certain that the use of a revoked certificate indicates malicious activity.
- Modify Application Data: Data from an untrusted (and possibly malicious) source may be integrated.
- Read Application Data: Data may be disclosed to an entity impersonating a trusted entity, resulting in information disclosure.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Ensure that certificates are checked for revoked status before each use of a protected resource. If the certificate is checked before each access of a protected resource, the delay subject to a possible race condition becomes almost negligible and significantly reduces the risk associated with this issue.
