# CWE-1465: Improper Session Invalidation after User Logout

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-1465** (Variant).

The product provides a logout functionality or otherwise attempts to end an active session for a user, but it does not properly invalidate the session upon logout.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-1464](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-1464.md)

## Consecuencias

- Gain Privileges or Assume Identity: Failure to invalidate a session on log out allows an adversary who has compromised the session more time to take action on the compromised session than is necessary.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Ensure that user sessions are invalidated on the server-side after a client logs out or attempts to end their session. It is important that the server-side handles all session invalidation – including multiple tabs or windows, such as within a web browser - and does not rely on the client to remove or delete a session due to the possibility of adversaries capturing session identifiers or cookies for later reuse.
