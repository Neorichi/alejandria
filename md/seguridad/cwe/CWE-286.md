# CWE-286: Incorrect User Management

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-286** (Class).

The product does not properly manage a user within its environment.

Users can be assigned to the wrong group (class) of permissions.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-284](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-284.md)

## Consecuencias

- Varies by Context: Users may be given unintended access rights to sensitive objects, or assigned to the wrong group (class) of permissions.
