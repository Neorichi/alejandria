# CWE-653: Improper Isolation or Compartmentalization

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-653** (Class).

The product does not properly compartmentalize or isolate functionality, processes, or resources that require different privilege levels, rights, or permissions.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-657](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-657.md), [CWE-693](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-693.md)

## Consecuencias

- Gain Privileges or Assume Identity: The exploitation of a weakness in low-privileged areas of the software can be leveraged to reach higher-privileged areas without having to overcome any additional obstacles, extending the scope of the damage to higher-privileged users..

## Cómo evitarlo

- Break up privileges between different modules, objects, or entities. Minimize the interfaces between modules and require strong access control between them.
