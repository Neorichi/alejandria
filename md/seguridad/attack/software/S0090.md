# S0090 Rover (Malware)

**Malware de MITRE ATT&CK** S0090.

- **Plataformas:** Windows

Rover is malware suspected of being used for espionage purposes. It was used in 2015 in a targeted email sent to an Indian Ambassador to Afghanistan.
