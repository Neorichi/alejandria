# CWE-511: Logic/Time Bomb

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-511** (Base).

The product contains code that is designed to disrupt the legitimate operation of the product (or its environment) when a certain time passes, or when a certain logical condition is met.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-506](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-506.md)

## Consecuencias

- DoS: Crash, Exit, or Restart: When the time bomb or logic bomb is detonated, it may perform a denial of service such as crashing the system, deleting critical data, or degrading system response time.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Always verify the integrity of the product that is being installed.
