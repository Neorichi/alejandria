# CWE-841: Improper Enforcement of Behavioral Workflow

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-841** (Class).

The product supports a session in which more than one behavior must be performed by an actor, but it does not properly ensure that the actor performs the behaviors in the required sequence or timeframe.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-691](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-691.md)

## Consecuencias

- Alter Execution Logic: By performing actions in an unexpected order, or by omitting steps, an attacker could manipulate the business logic of the product or cause it to enter an invalid state. In some cases, this can also expose resultant weaknesses with security implications.
