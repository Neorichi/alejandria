# CWE-1283: Mutable Attestation or Measurement Reporting Data

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-1283** (Base).

The register contents used for attestation or measurement reporting data to verify boot flow are modifiable by an adversary.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-284](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-284.md)

## Consecuencias

- Modify Memory: An adversary could spoof the measurement.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Measurement data should be stored in registers that are read-only or otherwise have access controls that prevent modification by an untrusted agent.
