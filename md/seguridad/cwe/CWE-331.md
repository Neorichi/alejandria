# CWE-331: Insufficient Entropy

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-331** (Base).

The product uses an algorithm or scheme that produces insufficient entropy, leaving patterns or clusters of values that are more likely to occur than others.

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-330](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-330.md)

## Consecuencias

- Bypass Protection Mechanism: An attacker could guess the random numbers generated and could gain unauthorized access to a system if the random numbers are used for authentication and authorization.

## Cómo evitarlo

- Determine the necessary entropy to adequately provide for randomness and predictability. This can be achieved by increasing the number of bits of objects such as keys and seeds.
