# CWE-568: finalize() Method Without super.finalize()

**Tipo de fallo de software CWE-568** (Variant).

The product contains a finalize() method that does not call super.finalize().

- **Es un caso de:** [CWE-573](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-573.md), [CWE-459](/seguridad/cwe/CWE-459.md)

## Consecuencias

- Quality Degradation

## Cómo evitarlo

- Call the super.finalize() method.
